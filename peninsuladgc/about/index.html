Our Mission

Building a thriving disc golf community

The Peninsula Disc Golf Club is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization founded in 2014 by a small group of passionate players who saw the potential for disc golf to become a cornerstone of outdoor recreation on the Peninsula.

Our mission is to promote, develop, and sustain disc golf on the Peninsula through community events, course stewardship, youth education, and advocacy for new course development. We believe disc golf has a unique power to bring people together across generations and backgrounds.

Every dollar raised through membership dues, tournament entry fees, and donations goes directly back into the community — maintaining existing courses, funding new installations, and supporting programs that put discs in the hands of young players.
