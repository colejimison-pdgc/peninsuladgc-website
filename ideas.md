## Selected Design: **Outdoor Adventure Editorial**

Chosen for its warm, community-focused feel that suits a non-profit disc golf club. The editorial layout gives the Events Calendar and Blog sections strong visual hierarchy, while the moss green + sunset orange palette evokes the outdoors without feeling generic. The tactile textures and Fraunces display font give the site personality and distinction.
